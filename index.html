About

Vision: become a noticeable name in security — a name vendors and researchers know — earned through published credits, not claimed. I pursue that from my own research lab (RL), not a classroom: credits first, noise later.

My main corner is Android & device security. I build in the Android / scrcpy ecosystem, so I research it too: device tooling, pairing and session models, and the trust boundaries between a phone, a host PC, and an AI agent. Leads are mapped from published source and verified locally, in my own lab and never outside a program's published scope — and logged, including the clean ones. Most leads turn out clean; working through them anyway is the discipline. Anything real goes privately to the maintainer first, with a proposed fix, never a public post before the vendor publishes.

Alongside research, I am a builder. On GitHub since 2015 · 60+ public repositories of AI tools, 3D editors, and voice-first systems under Vibin. And I run real systems: I am the third-generation owner-operator of Jodhpur Namkeen in Kota, a family business running since 1971. Operating a live business every day is a different kind of security education — you learn what downtime, fraud, and a broken integration actually cost, and why systems have to earn trust.

Credits, when they come, are listed on my security page with a link to the vendor's own page — evidence, not claims.

Approach

How I work — the responsible-disclosure discipline, borrowed from researchers who have earned vendor credits over a decade:

  1. Source-level review of published code, with AI as a co-pilot for mapping attack surface.
  2. Verify everything locally — in my own lab, never on systems outside a program's published scope.
  3. Coordinated disclosure every time: the vendor hears first and publishes first — and the fix patch ships with the report.
  4. Most leads turn out clean. Working through them anyway is the discipline — the clean ones get logged too.

Full method, research focus, and the credits ledger — updated only when a vendor publishes — live on the Security & Credits page.
