Research focus

  1. Primary

    Android & device tooling

    Security review of the Android / scrcpy / ADB tooling ecosystem I build on — pairing, session and trust models, host-side handling of device-supplied data.

  2. Secondary

    Web application security

    Authentication, session-state and access-control issues — tested only within a program's published scope, on my own accounts and in my own lab.

Method

  1. Source-level review of published code, with AI as a co-pilot for mapping attack surface.
  2. Coordinated disclosure every time: the vendor hears first and publishes first.
  3. Most leads turn out clean. Working through them anyway is the discipline — clean leads are logged, not hidden.
  4. Nothing unreported or unfixed is discussed publicly.

The lab

My research runs in my own lab in Kota, India: a multi-OS desk, my own devices, and the Android Bridge project as a live testbed for device-security ideas. Build and design notes: github.com/vaibhavjnf.
